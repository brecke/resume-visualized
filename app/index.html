A historical view of working time, year by year and discipline by discipline.

Years × disciplines

Percentages are exclusive shares of working time, not proficiency. Column labels identify disciplines, retaining their historical colors; intensity shows the share. White means a recorded 0%; full discipline color means 100%. Hatched cells marked “Unknown” have no recorded value.
